🐔 How to Play

  • Take turns rolling two dice (or tap Roll Dice in virtual mode). Every roll adds to the shared pot.
  • The first few rolls each round are safe — even a 7 just adds a bonus 70 to the pot.
  • After that, it's live: a 7 busts the round and the pot is lost for everyone still in, but a double instantly doubles the pot instead.
  • Tap 🐔 Chicken Out anytime to lock in the current pot as your score — but you're done for that round.
  • After all the rounds, whoever has the highest total score wins!
